# 📕 Lab Rules

[← Back to the knowledge base](README.md)

## General Rules

- This lab is a privilege and should be treated as such. Maintain a professional attitude while working in the lab.
- Cleanup is **MANDATORY** after you are done using the lab. The lab staff are not here to clean up after you; they are here to assist you in using the equipment.
- All the tools and materials are here for you to use, however, if you need to break, cut, or dismantle something that you haven't seen before, please ask the staff if it's ok to do so.
- Don't use hot glue or any permanent or semi-permanent glue, on electronic components such as development boards or breadboards. If you need to, please ask the staff if it's ok to do so.
- Please be mindful of the use of materials. If possible, try not to generate unnecessary waste and use the materials in the most efficient way.
- If you notice that the lab is short on any material or consumable that you feel might be needed in the future, please report it to the staff.
- If a tool doesn’t work properly or it gets damaged while using it please report it as soon as possible in order to fix it.
- Be mindful about disposing of things, don't throw away things that can be reused, fixed, or become prototyping material.
- When cleaning up your space, please separate the waste in the proper trash bins.
- No cups or open drinks in the lab.
- **There’s a surface for that. Make sure to use the right table, mat, or sheet for your task.**
- **Some things are lendable and some are not. If the equipment has a QR code, you can borrow it and return it by scanning the QR code**

## Safety Rules

- Use tools and safety protective equipment properly and return them to their assigned storage location after use.
- If you are not familiar with the use of a specific tool, please ask someone how to use it, the misuse of tools might result in accidents.
- Sanitize safety goggles after you use them.
- Any time a person is in the vicinity of a power tool or assisting someone using a power tool, they must be wearing proper eye and ear protection.
- Do not attempt to talk to or distract a person using a power tool, unless you think they are performing an unsafe operation. This can cause serious injury to both you and the operator.
- All injuries must be reported to lab staff.
- **NEVER** leave a machine unattended while it is still running.
- While using the equipment, listen to it. If it does not sound right, turn it off and get help from the lab staff.
- Always disconnect the power supply (unplug from the wall outlet) before servicing or changing blades.
- Fumes or dust extractors **MUST** be turned On while using the soldering stations, the wood machines, or other equipment that will produce fumes or dust.
- Stay alert. Never operate a power tool when you are tired or while under the influence of drugs, alcohol, or medication.
- **NEVER** operate any power tool if you are alone in the Lab, make sure always to have another person close by in case of an accident.
