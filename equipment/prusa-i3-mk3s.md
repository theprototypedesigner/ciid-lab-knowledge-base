## Technical specifications

The Original Prusa I3 MK3S is the successor of the award-winning Original Prusa i3 MK2 3D printer. With a rebuilt extruder, a plethora of sensors and the new magnetic MK52 heatbed with replaceable PEI spring steel print sheet.

- build volume – 11.025 cm3 (25 x 21 x 21 cm or 9,84 x 8,3 x 8,3 in)
- 0,4mm nozzle (easily changeable) for 1,75 mm filament
- Layer height from 0,05 mm
- Automatic mesh bed levelling
- Heatbed with cold corners compensation
- Automatic skew axes compensation
- Hassle free PEI print surface - no glass, no glue, no ABS juice
